The Blessings and the Misfortunes

The misfortunes, too weak to stand alone, once drove the blessings up into the sky. From there the blessings asked Zeus how they ought to reach human beings. He told them never to come all together, but to visit one at a time. And so the misfortunes, who live close at hand, fall upon us constantly, while the blessings, who must come down from heaven, arrive only now and then.

Moral:

This is why trouble reaches us so often, and good fortune so rarely.

Blessings were driven away by Misfortunes on the grounds that they were weak; they ascended to heaven. The Blessings asked Zeus how they might exist among humans.He replied that they should not all go together,but rather that each should come to humans one by one.For this reason, Misfortunes befall humans continuously,being close by,whereas good things arrive more slowly,descending from heaven.
